# Available-financial research eligibility — 2 November 2026

The previous research selector rejected every observed seven-check failure,
including a profitable current business whose comparison year was a loss.
The new, separately exported policy interprets current economics and lets
supplementary inputs remain unknown. It never changes a registered forecast,
fixed head, canonical financial fact, strict financial tier or evidence bar.

## Rules

* One dated canonical source with an original identity, URL and SHA-256 is
  sufficient. No second or third provider is required. Conflicting originals
  and owner/minority/total-PAT contradictions are quarantined, not averaged.
* Current identity and official source-price proof remain required. Use
  positive prices without a ₹20–₹2,000 band, simple rolling RSI14 **strictly
  above 70**, positive distance from the 50-session mean and the existing
  prior-run-up/risk constraints. Proximity within ±10% of the 200-session
  mean remains an optional ranking preference.
* Historical growth, all four profitable quarters, complete catalysts and
  forward valuation support are supplementary research inputs. Their absence
  does not prevent every candidate from being evaluated.
* Current-financial research requires a fresh reported quarterly revenue and
  PAT, annual operating cash flow, balance-sheet cash, equity and both
  borrowing components. Observed negative current profits/cash flow/cash,
  nonpositive equity, debt/equity above 1.5, inadequate observed interest
  cover and nonpositive reconciled owner PAT prevent selection.
* If essential facts are unknown but no adverse fact or source conflict is
  observed, a name may enter **incomplete financial discovery**. This never
  establishes profitability, financial verification or a lower loss risk.
* Zero annual finance cost is **not applicable** for interest coverage only
  when annual PBT is nonnegative and both current and annual-matched original
  balance sheets explicitly show zero current/noncurrent borrowings. The
  ratio remains missing; it is never replaced by infinity or a large number.
* A current positive PAT after a nonpositive year-ago quarter or annual PAT
  enters a separate **turnaround research** group when current safety facts
  are available. Export the monetary PAT change, not percentage growth over
  a loss base. Annual-to-quarter observations do not assert matched-period
  growth. QoQ sales/PAT changes are explicitly not seasonally adjusted.

## Outputs and uncertainty

`available_financial_research/latest.json` links an immutable all-name JSON
snapshot. Every input stock is retained, including unselected names, with
seven per-check states, missing-core fields, source links/hashes, periods,
actual information cutoffs, source conflict/weakness flags, selection reasons
and the exact original replay probabilities. At most 20 stocks per research
group are selected; no minimum number is forced.

The evidence score rewards observed passing checks and the strictly proven
zero-interest N/A. Unknown checks receive no evidence credit. The declared
ranking weights remain 75% joint-probability percentile, 10% financial
evidence, 5% complete execution, 5% supported net-2× scenario and 5% optional
200-DMA proximity. These are preferences, not fitted weights, calibrated
probabilities or a claim that missing data causes a specified extra loss.

All seven states can be classified while some remain unknown. Thus 100%
**classification** does not mean 100% observed inputs, 100% passing companies,
100% accuracy or verified forward earnings. The original six evidence
numerators and denominators retain their original definitions.

## Modeling and evaluation

The deployed V13 mixture already learns a median from the training subset and
appends a missing-value indicator for every retained feature. An observed
zero is distinct from a missing value; raw tables remain unchanged. There is
no need to introduce XGBoost or retrain a frozen head to enable that behavior.
The existing monthly experimental refits and maturity-purged four-year
validation continue; no new head is promoted by this eligibility change.

This is one small policy experiment: compare the legacy selector and the
new selector on the same source snapshot, head probabilities and **RSI >70**.
Report additions and removals, including those caused by current cash/debt
safety checks. A previously examined snapshot measures policy impact only;
it is not evidence of improved prediction accuracy. Retain all future
snapshots and use independently verified future entries/actions/outcomes
before accepting any improvement or replacing a frozen policy/model.

The exporter checks the exact replay input bytes, names, head hash and
probabilities. It writes the actual later policy-review time and cannot
retrofit a missing historical information cutoff. It makes no new formal
forecast registration or change to existing selected/unselected cohorts.

## Collection and operations

Missing financial facts remain in targeted collection. Nonpositive observed
denominators are interpretation tasks rather than fabricated download gaps;
the existing capture planner already omits denominator-only retries. Source
publication/review/retrieval clocks and identity checks remain mandatory.
Current-policy arithmetic uses the latest available versions at each actual
cutoff, preserving tied ambiguous versions, rather than rebuilding every
possible earlier filing-version combination. Source tables are not edited.

Worker and checkpoint exports include the new policy snapshots, while the
ordinary champion/challenger watchlists, frozen ledgers, monthly schedules and
future-outcome promotion gates remain separate. A failed optional policy
export is recorded as blocked diagnostics and is never called a success.

## Development impact check

On the previously examined 30 October price snapshot (1,636 stocks; reviewed
2 November), the same-RSI >70 legacy reference has 25 eligible names. The
new policy has 33: 29 with available current financials and four incomplete
discovery names. The per-group cap selects 20 plus four, and no turnaround
passes all market/risk rules. Eight names become eligible and none are
removed in this particular comparison. Nine zero-interest check positions
have the required explicit no-borrowing evidence. All 11,452 seven-check
positions receive an explicit state; missing/ambiguous inputs remain unknown.
These counts measure policy impact, not financial fact recovery or accuracy.
The existing strict six-area evidence bars are unchanged.
