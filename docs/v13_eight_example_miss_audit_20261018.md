The first 16 October snapshot had 3,185 current NSE securities, 2,281 stored
history files and 624 scored names. The subsequent 18 October refresh had
2,282 stored files, 887 with no 16 October bar, 144 with feature-ineligible
bars, one official OHLC mismatch excluded, and **1,250 scored**. Its 800
history attempts yielded 688 usable responses and 112 failures (39 insufficient
bars, 38 provider 404s, 35 symbol/venue validation). Missing bars account for a much larger missed-opportunity problem than
any single ranking threshold. The daily queue previously prioritised refresh
only for ₹20–₹2,000 shares despite removal of that range from the main gates;
that leftover rule is now replaced with a valid-price and rupee-turnover test.
This was a prioritisation bug, not proof that any one stale ticker was skipped
solely because of its nominal price.

The existing top-20 shadow base lane observes up to 756 preceding sessions,
requires at least 252 observed sessions and a 25–80% drawdown followed by a
bounded 30-session base. It deliberately relaxes RSI>70 and positive 50-DMA,
but does not waive real financial failure, missing prices, invalid probabilities,
or adverse project/dilution evidence. The attached daily worker audit now reads
the stored rolling history for these eight exact ISINs and reports first/last
bar, count, adjusted price range and drawdown, feature-eligible days, historical
trend/RSI overlap, observed bases, latest source-date and gate reason. It uses
the existing state after collection and changes no frozen forecasts.

The first refreshed run found 567 observed bases among 1,250 scored names,
195 eligible after the research safeguards, and 45 marked early recovery. Its
top 20 are a **shadow research watchlist**, not validated forecasts or buys.

The next 18 October refresh scored **1,636 of 3,185** NSE names with 2,283
stored histories. There remained 293 histories without the source-date bar,
351 with feature-ineligible bars, and three mismatches excluded after official
reconciliation. Among these eight examples, four were scored (EMUDHRA, DSSL,
CORONA, RSYSTEMS), one was stale and illiquid (RAJRILTD), and three were
current-NSE members with no stored history (PARAGON, AGOL, CHEMCRUX). The
shadow lane observed 725 bases, 256 eligible and 61 early recoveries across
the scored set. Both refreshes used the same 16 October market date.

**Limits and next decision.** The worker retains a rolling four-year window,
not all data since initial listing. A full since-listing reconstruction needs
validated corporate actions, historic exchange identities/series and older
venue-specific prices where applicable. These eight hand-picked screenshots create selection
bias; use prospective dated samples and matched risk-adjusted outcomes before
changing trained weights, risk limits, or claiming improved 2x prediction.
