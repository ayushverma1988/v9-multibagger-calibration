# V13 data capture improvement — 28 October 2026

The source-26 baseline is completed worker 40438162482: 374/613 financial
complete rows, 0/613 full execution, 0/613 source-supported net 2x, 4066/5300
verified entries, 0/5300 mature twelve-month outcomes, 613/3198 scored NSE
listings. The prior 417/666 measurement refers to a different source-day
population. Full-universe complete seven-check issuers rose 1284 to 1285.

The catalyst zero is a conjunction of completed capacity or a firm due-within-
twelve-month order, active execution, secured funding, requisite operating
approvals and a positive defined margin, together with financial completeness.
There are 4428 document records and nine partial bundles. Downloading another
PDF cannot alone prove the missing economic chain or future profit.

The baseline's 692 financial errors include 689 identity/rename failures,
one no-facts document, one 404 and one transient timeout. The new retry journal
retains unresolved originals but defers structural failures for seven days;
parser changes or a relevant issuer identity-reference change release them
earlier. Adding unrelated new issuers does not reset every failure. Access
limits keep their existing stop behavior. The journal is saved every 100
results and at completion. Actual attempts and retry dates are recorded.

The deployment raises filing requests 1600 to 2400 (900-second phase), catalyst
PDFs 120 to 360 (360-second phase), and API statement issuers 240 to 480
(300-second phase), retaining four workers and host spacing. The public
business-disclosure queue prioritises explicit order/capacity releases ahead of
administrative boilerplate while attempt age and a legal-risk reserve retain
rotation. No private forecast rank is supplied to sources.

A new cached-text pass extracts source-bound candidates for commercial
operation, commissioning, physical capacity and units, observed utilisation,
reported margin basis, funding, operating approvals, firm orders and absolute
execution dates. Hash and issuer checks, original publication/retrieval clocks
and the actual new extraction clock accompany every record. Duplicate original
documents are not counted as new sources. Conditional language, LOIs and legal
orders remain marked for context review. Issuer margin never becomes project
margin; extracted candidates never automatically complete chains or enter the
reviewed forecast feature ledger. This makes the review bottleneck visible and
removes repeated downloads for already cached texts.

The financial gap export distinguishes missing original periods/facts from
nonpositive mathematical denominators. For the source-26 baseline there are
530 missing/ambiguous field instances, 103 nonpositive-denominator instances
and 28 consolidated-quarter-gap instances across the 613 rows. These are
overlapping field counts, not 661 distinct issuers. Downloading cannot turn
growth from a prior loss, or interest cover at zero interest, into a valid
positive-denominator ratio. Underlying required raw facts are complete in
431/613 rows, compared with 374/613 complete ratios.

One duplicate full-universe financial audit is skipped when the same unchanged
financial snapshot was already audited earlier in the run. The full master
export, immutable forecast guard, action checks and final checkpoint remain.
Completion-plan ETAs read actual configured collection budgets.

This release changes collection and evidence diagnostics. Fixed heads,
registered probabilities/cohorts, thresholds, six/twelve-month targets and
promotion requirements are preserved. Tests and isolated source probes are
reported separately from measured coverage in a completed production worker.
Financial-source completion has no guaranteed calendar deadline; independently
mature future outcomes still need six/twelve months of actual market time.

## Measured quarter capture repair

Several original half-year statements contain cumulative H1 results but omit
the separate September-quarter context. The existing derivation handled only
annual minus nine months. It now also handles H1 minus Q1 and nine months
minus H1, requiring the same issuer, reporting basis and explicit fiscal start,
and a 60–120-day difference. Only additive monetary flows are subtracted;
EPS, shares, cash stocks and balance-sheet amounts are not. Explicit original
quarter statements keep precedence. Both input hashes/URLs, the later source
availability and the fact that independent revision comparability is unproven
remain attached. Canonical originals and registered model inputs are unchanged.

On the SAME source-26 population and original snapshot, an isolated recompute
increases financial completeness **374 to 408 of 613** (61.01% to 66.56%) and
quality passes **185 to 200**, with zero complete-row regressions. The 34 newly
complete rows are data-readiness gains, not 34 investment selections or proved
future winners. Old frozen forecasts were not rescored. The completed worker
artifact must independently verify deployed results on its own source day.

The separate live 200-request original-source probe accepted 164 filing versions
and extracted 2432 fact observations, mostly already present: net source-period
rows increased by seven and nonmissing numeric observations by sixty. Complete
whole-universe issuers stayed 1285. Thirty-four timeouts and two identity
conflicts were retained as failures; 576 structurally unresolved versions were
deferred rather than deleted. It reverified eight complete PDF/text receipts
from twenty attempts; eleven identical original PDFs had a different extraction
vintage and one request timed out. Four documents yielded seven source-bound
field candidates (five capacity, one utilisation and one firm-order statement).
None completes an execution chain. These honest incremental measurements are
separate from the quarter-repair comparison and production coverage.
