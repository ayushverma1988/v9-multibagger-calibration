# V13 financial completion and prospective evaluation — 13 October 2026

The paired forecast mechanism is already deployed. Its frozen 12 October
cohort contains 82 identical ISINs for each fixed model at the same actual
registration time. The 670 older unpaired forecasts remain unpaired; no
retrospective challenger predictions are manufactured.

## This revision

- Reserve 75% of the bounded financial collection budget for issuer bundles
  from the public collected-history universe, and 25% for broader rotation.
  Bundle seven recent fiscal endpoints on one reporting basis, prioritising
  issuers closest to complete evidence without consulting forecast ranks.
- Reparse accepted originals with the explicitly versioned YTD parser. Check
  a previously accepted version against its stored source hashes; changed
  bytes require revision review rather than silent historical replacement.
- Retain exact six/nine-month economic durations, currency units, URLs and
  retrieval clocks. Legacy parsing defaults stay unchanged.
- Where a separate March quarter is absent, derive annual minus nine-month
  monetary amounts only for the same issuer, reporting basis and fiscal
  start. Retain both input hashes/links and the later availability clock.
  Do not subtract EPS, share counts or cash-flow periods. An explicit quarter
  takes precedence. Balancing quarters are not recurring earnings or proof
  of historical revision integrity.
- Export explicit paired evaluation at both horizons, by financial tier,
  observed market breadth and liquidity. Include unselected names and the
  unknown denominator; do not report immature accuracy. Risk diagnostics
  continue to require verified loss-before-doubling outcomes.
- Version the input/selection comparison contract separately from head weights.
  Older policies remain tracked but cannot be pooled into promotion evidence
  for a changed policy. A future promotion activates the evaluated selection
  policy together with its weights, rather than promoting weights alone.
- Accept provider split-scaled historical OHLC only when original reviewed
  share actions explain the entire scaling sequence, independent total
  returns agree, and official entry/session/identity evidence matches. Bare
  provider factors cannot verify entries. Export unresolved action intervals
  for all committed cohorts and exclude orphan registrations.
- Add source-bound reviews of JNPR, ARSSINFRA and TATAPOWER disclosures.
  Units, incomplete certificates, unknown contract starts, subsidiary scope,
  margins and funding remain explicit. Review availability begins at the
  actual review clock, so new reviews do not rewrite an earlier forecast.
  Only financially complete execution chains with positive disclosed margins,
  verified sufficient funding and completed approvals can support the
  stricter evidence selection policy. Missing fields never become zero.
- Export reverse earnings/valuation/dilution arithmetic for the net 2× target.
  These are hypothetical requirements, not source-verified forward estimates
  or new probabilities. Issued shares are not weighted diluted shares.

## Public-source check

DEEDEV's seven required financial inputs became complete, but CFO/PAT was
about 0.2533: complete data exposed a quality failure rather than establishing
a buy. UGARSUGAR retained losses/debt problems. INDIANB needs a banking-specific
taxonomy, and earlier JSWDULUX documents require independent symbol-transition
mapping. Those gaps are not bypassed. The sample is an engineering source
check, not an unseen prediction validation or a replacement candidate list.

Fixed champion/challenger weights, registered probabilities, old cohorts,
monthly experimental refits and independently verified future promotion gates
remain intact. No trades or financial performance guarantees are introduced.
