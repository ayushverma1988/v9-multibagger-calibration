# V13 annual cash-flow screen

V13 uses annual operating cash flow divided by annual profit after tax of at least 0.35 for market source dates from 28 November 2026 onward. Earlier and undated screens retain the original 0.40 minimum. The complete financial audit, the prospective selection gates and both priority RSI lists use the same dated policy. Missing ratios are still unknown, and other financial and source checks continue to apply.

This is a change to selection eligibility, not the model's probability functions, learned weights or historical results. Both priority lists identify their applied cutoffs, and new paired cohort manifests record the applicable annual cash-flow floor. Existing registered market dates remain immutable. A what-if comparison using the 27 November close is an analysis, not a new registered forecast or validation of better performance.
