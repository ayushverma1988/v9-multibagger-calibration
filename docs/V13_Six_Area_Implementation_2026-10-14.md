## Implemented changes

1. **Dated financial inputs:** larger bounded collection budgets; prioritised quarterly bundle completion with a cold-start reserve; stable trailing-four-year backfill windows; owner-attributable PAT and minority interests kept separate; reviewed original filings reparsed without changing their source hashes. Nonpositive denominators remain known failures rather than being retried as missing inputs.
2. **Quantified catalysts:** evidence joins require an explicit identical project or order identity. Cancellation and later contradictory evidence override older assertions. Commercial operation, operating approvals, secured funding and a positive defined margin are required for a complete execution chain. Project utilisation is not an issuer average. A five-year order, an undated 18-month concession, a letter of intent, and planned capacity are not certified twelve-month revenue or profit.
3. **Valuation support:** original dated NSE P/E reports joined to official prices and industry membership; peer samples exclude the issuer and require five other positive-P/E peers. Reported P/E and hypothetical earnings/re-rating hurdles are supplemental research evidence, not recurring diluted EPS proof or source-supported forward earnings. Previously missing scenarios remain unknown.
4. **Entries and corporate actions:** original exchange notice collection; source hashes and recorded observation clocks; daily low/high reconciliation with official unadjusted prices and provider adjustment factors. Frozen risk probabilities retain their original loss-before-doubling target. Any-horizon loss and price-only doubling are reported separately. Same-day loss/doubling is conservatively treated as loss first. Dividend cash cannot certify price-only doubling.
5. **Fair future evaluation:** champion/challenger continue to register identical dated stock cohorts with frozen probabilities, baselines and distinct comparison contracts. Evidence coverage, tier, market regime and liquidity groups remain explicit. Added owner-earnings and cash-quality experiments abstain when fewer than 100 usable financial observations exist. Historical experiments are previously examined research and cannot authorise promotion. A runtime integrity guard verifies old registries, head files and original observation fields, restoring protected originals before a failure checkpoint if mutation is detected.
6. **NSE breadth:** four-worker bounded collection, staggered requests, phase time budgets and a stop on rate-limit/forbidden responses; public liquidity/history priorities plus a cold-start reserve. Warm coverage is prioritised without using private picks or future outcomes. Coverage is measured against the actual dated public NSE universe.

For new cohorts, the information cutoff is the actual observation time, capped at 22:00 IST on the price date; it must precede registration. Original cohorts retain their original clocks and contracts. Existing source/head pairs are idempotent and cannot be enlarged retrospectively. Entry remains the first eligible next-session official open, with incomplete corporate-action evidence flagged.
