# V13 dated evidence and paired prospective evaluation

This extension prioritises financial/catalyst completeness and fair evaluation. It does not approve accuracy or rewrite initial forecasts.

## Paired protocol

The worker creates the initial fixed challenger before committing paired forecasts. Champion and challenger use the same official-price-matched, trailing-four-year input snapshot, actual UTC registration clock, security identities and source date. Every scoreable stock is recorded, including unselected stocks. Original unpaired registries remain immutable and cannot be retrospectively paired. A hash-bound cohort manifest commits both registries; repeat collection on the same source date cannot revise a cohort. Missing conditional heads cannot register a successful forecast. Optional missing watchlists are not new forecasts.

A simple smoothed, maturity-purged event-rate baseline is frozen alongside available predictions. Paired maturity and source coverage have an explicit empty schema; no missing or immature outcome is relabelled zero. Both models must share entry dates and independent labels. Promotion counts complete elapsed cohorts, including unknown/delisted observations, rather than discarding inconvenient outcomes. Six distinct mature months, adequate positives/selections, date-clustered gains and complete independent sources remain required.

## Financial and catalyst collection

Financial queues rotate through the public issuer catalogue, prioritising missing recent quarter/annual periods and issuer diversity. An original filing with no usable exact-identity facts remains queued. Per-stock missing-input reports and original financial source links are exported.

Whole-venue announcement windows backfill two bounded 29-day windows per run inside the trailing four-year interval. Original catalyst PDFs are mined for quantified quantities and execution/funding/margin/approval snippets. Automatic extraction creates a review backlog, not verified future earnings. Reviewed fields bind to original hashes, issuer identities and exact excerpts. Plans cannot count as commissioning. Unknown publication times use first retrieval, not midnight on a printed date. Source-bound Aeroflex evidence reports July 2026 commissioning to 9,000 pieces/year; utilisation, profit, funding and final planned capacity remain unknown.

Reported TTM PAT, latest diluted shares and implied PE support earnings-feasibility arithmetic when available. Reported EPS is not recurring EPS. Forward profit/share-count/sector-PE inputs require explicit source-linked scenario records. Missing assumptions remain unknown. Doubling scenarios include the registered 0.5% cost per side and dilution; scenarios are not probabilities.

## Primary entries and corporate actions

Official NSE calendar and missing-session snapshots support entry checks. Original split/bonus/dividend notices, complete reviewed action intervals, official unadjusted prices and provider adjustment ratios must reconcile before an outcome is independently confirmed. Rights, mergers/demergers and symbol/ISIN transitions require dedicated mapping and remain unknown. Raw early doubling alerts are separate from confirmed events and full six/twelve-month metrics. Original hashes alone do not prove economic correctness.

## Selection and research

The frozen champion's weights and original selections are preserved. The challenger evidence policy may select zero stocks: it requires complete available financial checks, reviewed execution evidence and an explicit earnings/valuation scenario supporting a net 2x. Qualified stocks rank by the existing joint probability of doubling before a 30% adverse move; lists are not padded to 20. This policy is a predeclared research preference, not an established accuracy gain.

Prospective metrics cover all registered observations, by financial tier, observed-cohort market breadth and liquidity. Coverage is partial NSE, so these breadth labels are not claimed whole-market regimes. Accuracy numbers remain absent until independent mature labels exist.

Monthly research refits remain separate from the frozen formal challenger. Three predeclared small feature groups (market; market+financial; market+financial+catalyst) are evaluated against the prior-rate baseline on up to three latest matured monthly holdouts per horizon. Training labels must mature before each test decision; imputation uses training data only. Previously examined history is development evidence. Sparse quantified catalysts block their experiment instead of becoming synthetic zeros. Monthly failures retain fixed models and report the failure.

## Remaining data restrictions

Engineering enables collection and review; it does not fill all financial histories or certify all corporate actions immediately. Recurring EPS, many executable margin/funding bridges, sector-relative valuation and full independent action intervals remain incomplete until sources supply them. Financial/catalyst backlog counts and independent proof status are persisted in each run. Future six/twelve-month results must mature in calendar time.
