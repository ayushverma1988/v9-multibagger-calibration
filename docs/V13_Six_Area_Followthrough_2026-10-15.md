# Six-area evidence follow-through — 15 October 2026

This release improves collection and prospective verification. It does not
change frozen forecasts, fixed champion/challenger heads, old cohorts,
probabilities, selected/unselected memberships or promotion requirements.
Data and future accuracy are not declared complete by passing software tests.

## Changes

1. **Dated financial inputs:** reserve up to one quarter of the existing 800
   filing requests for missing consolidated owner-attributable earnings in the
   current, year-ago and remaining TTM quarters. The basic seven checks retain
   a separate bundle budget, and at least a quarter remains for cold expansion.
   Queue priority uses the unranked public price-eligible population, never
   private model selections. An observed zero owner result is not a missing
   original; standalone or total consolidated PAT cannot replace owner PAT.
   Recurring EPS still requires separate evidence.
2. **Catalyst execution:** add three independently reviewed original company
   notices to the existing source seed. Navin Fluorine explicitly reports
   commercial production/dispatch commencing that day at the Dahej MPP
   debottlenecking project. Aris's Transcon UNO service engagement has an
   eighteen-month initial term: INR400 crore is project GDV, not fees or
   company revenue. Waaree Renewable's INR14.79 crore scope revision belongs
   to the existing 2012.47 MWp project and is not an additive second award.
   Unknown margins/funding/operating approvals remain unknown. Installation
   records its actual review time; nothing enters an earlier frozen cohort.
3. **Earnings/valuation support:** export a six-area completion plan with
   explicit revenue-to-recurring-owner-earnings, forward PAT period, dilution,
   justified valuation, entry/source-price and transaction-cost dependencies.
   The existing source-bound scenario validator remains in force. Current
   reported P/E, project GDV and hypothetical EPS hurdles do not make a
   source-supported net-2x scenario.
4. **Independent entries/actions:** persist verified entry open, primary price
   hash, provider hash and observation time into non-forecast ledger fields.
   Rotate failed action-document requests behind unattempted originals so
   oversized files cannot monopolise every batch. Neither notices nor raw
   price files certify a complete no-action interval.
5. **Fair evaluation:** matching entry dates alone no longer establishes
   matching entry evidence. Paired evaluation requires both verified flags,
   positive matching official opens (INR0.011 tolerance) and identical valid
   primary/provider hashes. Comparison contracts and information cutoffs must
   match. A frozen baseline must be present and equal on both sides before
   baseline comparisons; missing entries/baselines remain unknown. These
   requirements apply to promotion too. Old missing proof is not invented or
   retroactively inserted into registered forecasts. Monthly refits remain
   experimental and previously examined diagnostics cannot replace fixed heads.
6. **NSE breadth:** add up to eight completed-session, whole-public-market
   official price downloads per run toward a 127-session raw reference window,
   within four years. Requests are bounded, restartable and stop on throttling.
   This supplies independent identity/OHLC/action comparisons beyond the
   registration window. Unadjusted raw history is supplemental: it does not
   become adjusted model history or increase scored NSE coverage automatically.
