# V13 prediction research adoption — 3 November 2026

The requested adoption improves the research objective, diagnosis and comparison
process. It does not establish that a new model predicts future doubles more
accurately. Registered forecasts, the fixed champion and formal challenger remain
unchanged. No historical experiment can replace their independent future outcomes.

## Research behavior

- The additional six-month research list ranks 70% by six-month doubling estimate
  percentile, 25% by twelve-month joint-outcome percentile and 5% by optional
  proximity to the 200-session mean. It reports at most 25 candidates, preserves
  evidence labels and never fills the list with failing names. The ranking score
  is not a probability. Existing four-head probabilities remain attached unchanged.
- Strict RSI14 >70, no nominal price band and optional 200-DMA proximity are
  retained from the latest deployed policy. The original dated RSI >=50 policy is
  historical. The RSI calculation remains a simple rolling 14-session version.
- Bank and insurer assessments no longer reject an issuer using industrial
  leverage or cash-flow ratios. Dated identity-bound sector evidence and original
  banking filing types govern classification. Positive ROA, capital buffer and
  non-worsening net NPA are required for the available-current-financial bank tier;
  missing sector metrics remain discovery data gaps. No bank XML facts are invented.
- Whole histories are joined by symbol, ISIN and session. A conflicting security
  is quarantined individually; unrelated names remain usable. Historical aliases
  cannot silently supply another symbol's features. Refits retain unique archived
  historical-only identities where available and quarantine ambiguous aliases.

## Matched historical research

`python -m v13_policy_evaluation --matrix MATRIX --current CURRENT --out OUTPUT
--asof YYYY-MM-DD --max-dates 12` compares:

1. A single-date calibration control.
2. Two-date regularized calibration, separate from blend selection.
3. Market-only features.
4. Full features excluding the explicit catalyst feature group.
5. Explicit breakout, compression, volume and extension features.
6. Context measured against a fixed reference population sensitivity.

Every variant uses matched dates, four conditional heads, matured-only labels and
chronological purging before training, blend selection and calibration. Historical
selection drops the removed nominal price band. Exact primary NSE session matches
are required for reported eligible selections. Current twelve-month-priority,
six-month-priority and a lower-risk-ceiling sensitivity are compared at K=5/20/25.
Global K replaces production group caps for this matched experiment; unavailable
historical execution/valuation bonuses receive no fabricated credit.

Reports include Brier score, log loss, average precision, calibration bins,
precision at K, twelve-month mature counts, six-month loss events and endpoint
returns. Six-month-block resampling reports paired monthly precision uncertainty.
The source matrix hash, original preparation run, decision dates and training
clocks accompany the output. Experimental heads are saved separately.

These are previously examined developmental data. Current-listing survivor bias,
provider-adjustment vintage limitations, incomplete original historical filing
vintages and unverified full corporate-action intervals remain disclosed. A fixed
reference sensitivity does not repair the historical investable universe. The
prepared data snapshot predates the new sector overlay; the first benchmark must
not be described as validating those sector changes. Model promotion remains
closed, and no 24-month probabilities are created.

## Collector continuity

The observed private-archive quota failure is handled only when the explicit
artifact fallback is enabled. Recovery manifests, sizes and all original hashes
are checked before restoration. Existing archive pointers stay unchanged until a
real remote publication succeeds. A locally prepared delta is labelled pending
GitHub artifact upload; it is not reported as an archive commit. Other integrity,
permission and transport failures are not swallowed by the quota fallback.

The existing daily worker schedule remains unchanged. Paid archive capacity is
still an account constraint; no upgrade, deletion or credential exposure occurs.
GitHub artifact retention is finite and is not permanent archival capacity.

## Evidence basis

Repeated historical strategy searches can overfit; the comparison therefore
records all declared variants and does not promote the best examined result as a
validated model. See Bailey et al., *The Probability of Backtest Overfitting*:
https://escholarship.org/uc/item/4w1110bb.

John Bollinger's own rules treat band touches as context, not standalone trade
signals, and default parameters as defaults. Chart features stay measurable
experimental inputs rather than guaranteed multibagger patterns:
https://www.bollingerbands.com/bollinger-band-rules.
