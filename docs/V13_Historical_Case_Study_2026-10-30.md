# Historical multibagger cases — fixed exploratory intake

The user supplied ten retrospective winners on 30 October 2026: KERNEX,
AEGISLOG, CGPOWER, TBZ, PGEL, APOLLO, CUPID, TARIL, KPIGREEN and JWL.
The exact quoted prices, identities, source links and unresolved dates are
preserved in `v13_historical_cases_20261030.json`. APOLLO means Apollo Micro
Systems, as identified by NSE's face-value split circular CML56477.

## Interpretation of the quoted returns

Endpoint divided by starting price gives a claimed price multiple, conditional
on a comparable share basis. No price date, adjustment convention or realised
investment return was supplied. None is invented. A 2020–2026 move is not proof
of a 2x opportunity from any particular six- or twelve-month entry. Picking a
historic low after seeing a later high is not an executable entry rule.

Official records already establish material adjustment questions:

- APOLLO: face value 10 to 1 effective 4 May 2023 (NSE CML56477).
- PGEL: face value 10 to 1 effective 10 July 2024 (NSE CML62700).
- CUPID: NSE CML73156 records the 9 March 2026 bonus record date and 10 March
  deemed allotment; its 29 January issuer announcement specifies 4 new shares
  for each existing share. Earlier actions still need full reconciliation.
- TARIL: the issuer reports a 1:1 bonus with record date 14 February 2025 and
  allotment on 17 February 2025.
- KPIGREEN: MSE's exchange action history includes multiple bonuses and a
  face-value change. This is an audit lead, not a complete interval certificate.
- JWL: the issuer's December 2023 placement document describes the amalgamation
  with CEBBCO and 2022 name change. Earlier business/financial comparability and
  historical security aliases require review.

Full corporate-action intervals for all ten remain unverified. Split/bonus
issuance itself is not operating wealth creation. Dividends, rights, mergers,
changes in shares, record dates and trading entitlements must be reconstructed
before certifying investor returns. Adjustment factors must not be applied
again to a provider series that already includes them.

## Reproducible first study

The read-only case workflow downloads public Yahoo Finance daily histories
from 2018 for indicator warm-up and analyses 2020 through 29 October 2026. NSE's
current official master binds present symbol/ISIN identities; it does not prove
the whole historical identity chain. Both original responses and SHA-256
receipts are retained. Provider metadata, split/dividend events and rejected
rows are exported. Access limitations remain explicit, with no fabricated bars.

The first capture found KPIGREEN's NSE history starts on its official NSE
listing date, 27 July 2021. Its earlier BSE security code 542323 is identified
by the exchange-hosted issuer filing in the intake sources. A separate BSE
panel and SENSEX session calendar cover pre-NSE decision dates; no automatic
cross-venue splice or historical identity certificate is claimed. Requested
cutoffs and actual latest returned price dates are reported separately.

Every completed calendar month supplies its last observed session as a fixed
decision. Indicators use only prior/current observations. Entries use the next
session open, checked against the downloaded NIFTY 50 session calendar. Missing
first sessions or internal sessions produce UNKNOWN labels. Incomplete future
horizons remain IMMATURE. Neither becomes a negative example.

Six and twelve **calendar months** are evaluated separately. The existing 0.5%
cost per side convention is applied. Closing dividend-adjusted values provide
only a provisional total-return proxy; adjusted daily lows measure the 30%
risk event. A day containing both a target close and a low loss event is
conservatively loss-first. Slippage, actual gap fills, circuit restrictions,
entitlements and complete action intervals remain unverified. Zero-volume
equity rows are rejected rather than treated as executable sessions.

Outputs include simple 14-session RSI, price above the 50-session mean,
50/200-session mean relationship, distance to the 200-session mean, prior
63-close breakout and volume relative to the previous 20 sessions. These are
diagnostic fields, not newly validated filters. The current inclusive RSI >=50
policy and optional 200-DMA proximity are recorded; no production filter,
frozen model, forecast, financial master or learning weight is changed.

## Business evidence still required

Reconstruct information that was public **before each decision**, including:
quarterly sales and recurring diluted owner EPS, margin changes, CFO/PAT,
working capital, ROCE, interest coverage, shareholder dilution and dated
valuation. Link capacity/order announcements to actual execution deadlines,
funding, approvals, utilisation and margins. Restated or current financial pages
must not be passed off as what an investor knew at an earlier date.

The business questions below are hypotheses for source review, not established
causes of returns:

| Cases | Main hypothesis to investigate |
|---|---|
| KERNEX, APOLLO, JWL | Firm orders, execution timing, cash conversion and capacity bottlenecks |
| CGPOWER, TARIL | Recovery/operating change, governance, order-to-profit conversion and dilution |
| PGEL | Manufacturing scale, utilisation, margins, working capital and cash conversion |
| AEGISLOG, KPIGREEN | Commissioned assets, funding, utilisation, recurring cash flow and valuation |
| CUPID, TBZ | Business mix, ownership/governance changes, earnings quality and valuation |

## Admission and evaluation gates

These names were selected after their outcomes were known. They can help form
questions and inspect missed opportunities. They cannot by themselves estimate
precision, calibration or a future doubling probability. Repeated monthly
entries and overlapping six/twelve-month outcomes are not independent samples.

Before testing new rules, fix a historical universe containing failures,
non-winners, delisted/renamed securities and tradability constraints known on
each decision date. Match controls using only then-known sector, size and
liquidity, rather than later performance. Use date-separated evaluation with
mature labels and purge overlapping outcome windows. Compare the existing
model, an unchanged technical baseline and each proposed addition on the same
stock-date population. Record misses and false positives, drawdowns, net
returns, coverage and uncertainty, not only successful chart examples.

2020 is explicitly labelled as a stress/rebound case-study year. Supplying these
examples does not automatically reintroduce the COVID crash into the formal
training folds. The existing exclusion is preserved. Independent prospective
validation and the original frozen forecast clocks remain unchanged.
